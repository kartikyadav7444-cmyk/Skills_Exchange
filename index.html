How SkillExchange Works

A frictionless learning flow designed for college students to share knowledge without administrative hurdles.

1

Create Your Profile

Sign up with your college year and branch. Select the skills you are confident to teach, and the skills you are eager to learn.

2

Discover Peers

Browse student cards or search by specific skill. Filter by learning mode (Online, Offline, or Both) to find matching classmates.

3

Send Learning Request

Request a learning session with one click. There is no strict barter—you can learn from someone even if you aren't teaching them back.

4

Learn & Leave a Review

Meet online or on campus. After your doubt-clearing session, provide a friendly 1–5 star rating and review to appreciate their help.

Why Student Skill Exchange?

Traditional learning often isolates students. Here is how peer-to-peer exchange transforms campus life.

Cross-Year Connections

Break the departmental and year silos. First-year students can comfortably seek guidance from experienced seniors without hesitation or intimidation.

Immediate Doubt Clarification

No need to wait for weekly faculty office hours. Classmates explain concepts in relatable, plain language with student-level examples.

Flexible Exchange Network

Unlike a rigid barter system, Student A can teach Student B without Student B having to teach Student A in return. Teach what you love, learn what you need.

100% Free & Accessible

College students shouldn't have to pay high fees for basic software or programming tips. Campus knowledge stays within campus, freely accessible to all.

Reinforce Your Own Skills

Teaching someone else is the most effective way to master a subject. Seniors polish their fundamentals while preparing junior peers.

Verified Student Ratings

Transparent, genuine peer ratings and reviews build mutual trust and highlight the most helpful mentors in your campus community.

Online & On-Campus Learning

Students can choose whatever learning mode fits their timetable and comfort level.

Online Sessions

  • - Exchange notes, code snippets, and presentation slides

FAQs

What is SkillSwap?

SkillSwap is a student-to-student learning network for your campus. You list the skills you can teach and the ones you want to learn, and the platform helps you find classmates and seniors who can help.

Is SkillSwap really free to use?

Yes. There are no fees and no payments between students. SkillSwap is built on mutual growth, so you never spend a single rupee.

Do I have to teach something in order to learn?

No. There is no pressure of a direct one-to-one exchange. You can join just to learn, just to teach, or do both. Sharing what you know is encouraged, but not required.

How do I get in touch with another student?

Search for a skill, open a student's profile and click the connect button. Once they accept your request, their contact details unlock and you can plan a study session in the library, in the hostel or online.

Who can join the platform?

Any student on campus, from any academic year or branch. A first-year student can learn from a senior in a different department, and the other way around.

Is my contact information safe?

Your contact details stay hidden until you accept a connection request, so only students you agree to connect with can see them.
